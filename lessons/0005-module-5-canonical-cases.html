The Three Canonical Cases

1 · Baseline ✓ 2 · Five-line bill ✓ 3 · CfD mechanics ✓ 4 · Three gates ✓ 5 · Canonical cases 6 · Decisions

Every DPPA hour is one of three shapes: load = gen (matched), load > gen (shortfall), or load < gen (excess). Same five-line math, three outcomes. This module is the live tool you'll demo — these are its three scenarios.

1 · The three shapes

What settles, what buys retail, and what earns the generator nothing — set by the load/gen overlap.
Teal = factory load · amber = solar · gray = shortfall (bought retail) · dashed = excess (generator spot only, no CfD).

2 · What each case costs vs BAU

All three are a Year-1 premium over retail; the payoff is certainty as EVN escalates.
Shortfall (B) shows the lowest blended premium — its gap kWh buy at retail (2,204), diluting the matched-kWh premium.

Check yourself

2. In all three cases, Year-1 DPPA cost is ___
At a bankable strike (2,000), every case is a Year-1 premium (+11.5% to +16.7%); value builds as EVN escalates.
3. Multi-party netting equals the ___
Net CfD = Σ per-pair (Strike − FMP) × matched. The same five-line bill runs per pair, in parallel.
