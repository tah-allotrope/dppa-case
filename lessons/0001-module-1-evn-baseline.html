The Baseline: What You Pay EVN Today

1 · Baseline 2 · Five-line bill 3 · CfD mechanics 4 · Three gates 5 · Canonical cases 6 · Decisions

Before any DPPA math, you need the thing it's measured against: your EVN bill. Two facts about that bill decide everything downstream — it's time-of-use, and it escalates.

The one idea

A virtual DPPA is not a Year-1 discount. You're judged against a BAU bill that rises ~4%/yr — so the DPPA's value is the widening gap between EVN's escalating tariff and your locked-in strike, measured over 10–25 years.

Fact 1 — Your bill is time-of-use (TOU)

EVN charges three rate bands across the day. Peak energy costs about 3.4× off-peak. So your load shape — when you actually run — decides what "average price" even means for you. A night-shift factory and a 9-to-5 factory pay very different averages for the same kWh total.

Fact 2 — The benchmark is a moving target

BAU = your current EVN bill, escalated ~4%/yr (the 2015–2024 trend). That's the line a DPPA must beat — not today's price, but the rising curve. Because EVN keeps climbing while a DPPA strike is locked (or escalates slower), the savings accumulate.

The trap to avoid Judging a DPPA on Year-1 price. In most realistic structures Year-1 DPPA cost is at or above BAU. Evaluate on cumulative 10-year and lifetime cost vs BAU.

Why this matters for the rest of the deck

  • Residual stays on TOU. Any load the DPPA doesn't cover (shortfall) is bought from EVN at retail — that's line 4 of the five-line bill (Module 2).
  • Load shape is king. Hour-by-hour overlap of your load and the solar profile decides how much settles — the heart of Module 3 and the three canonical cases.
  • Escalation is the value. The buyer "gate" (Module 4) is literally "cumulative cost ≤ BAU over 10 yr and lifetime."
