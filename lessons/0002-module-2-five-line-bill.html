Virtual DPPA Money Flow: The Five-Line Bill

1 · Baseline ✓ 2 · Five-line bill 3 · CfD mechanics 4 · Three gates 5 · Canonical cases 6 · Decisions

A virtual DPPA changes no electrons. It's a financial overlay: you still pay EVN, and a contract-for-difference (CfD) settles separately with the generator. Your total cost = a four-line EVN bill + one CfD line.

The money map

Physical electrons flow through the grid as usual; the DPPA rides on top as money.
Generator revenue = spot sales (FMP) + CfD. Your cost = EVN bill (lines 1–4) + CfD (line 5).

Which volume do you pay on?

You settle only on what you actually consume — contracting above that buys nothing.
Q_adj = Q_meter ÷ K_pp · Q_Khc = min(Load, Q_adj) · Q_CfD = min(Q_c, Q_Khc).

Worked example — 50 MW, fully matched

6,000,000 kWh/mo · FMP 1,200 · strike 1,300 · no residual. (Source: EAVCED training.)

Market energy dominates; the 523 fixed fees are ~28%; CfD is a thin ±slice. EVN bill = 10.59B, +CfD 0.60B = 11.19B.
Don't over-generalize this example Here effective cost (1,864) beats retail (2,204) — but only because the strike (1,300) sits just 100 above FMP, which no lender would finance (Module 4). With a bankable strike (~2,000), Year-1 is a premium — exactly Module 1's point.

Check yourself

1. The 523 VND/kWh of DPPA fees is ___
Lines 2+3 (360 + 163.3) are regulated, charged on every matched kWh regardless of strike. Only the strike is negotiable.
2. Line 5 (CfD) settles on ___
Q_CfD = min(Q_c, Q_Khc). Contracting above what you consume buys nothing; excess earns the generator spot only.
3. In line 1, k and K_pp together equal ___
k (1.026, price conversion) × K_pp (1.008, loss) = 1.0342. App stores it as lossFactor.
