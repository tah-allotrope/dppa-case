2 · Where the money goes

Generator revenue = 5.8B spot + 0.5B CfD = 6.3B. Factory cost = 8.5B EVN bill + 0.5B CfD = 9.06B.

3 · Which way does the CfD flow?

FMP (1,150) is below strike (1,250) → CfD is positive → the factory tops the developer up to the strike.

Check yourself

1. With consumption = matched volume, line 4 (residual) is ___
No shortfall → (QKH − Qkhhc) = 0 → line 4 = 0. All consumption is covered by matched RE.
2. C_EVN (lines 1–4) for Scenario 1 sums to ___
5,946,696,000 + 1,800,000,000 + 816,500,000 + 0 = 8,563,196,000. (9.06B is C_KH, after the CfD.)
3. Because FMP is below the strike, the CfD means the factory ___
CfD = (Strike − FMP) × Q = +500M; positive means the factory pays the developer up to the strike.
