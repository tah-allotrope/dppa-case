## Problem & Why Now
The CEBA Vietnam Day-2 panel already has a **drafted 22-question bank** (`ceba/CEBA 2026_Panel Questions.md`) — bilingual EN/VI, sectioned, with a tentative bench (VinEnergo, EVNGENCO3, Samsung C&T, BIDV) and a stated theme of *risk allocation and the reality of bank financing for Grid DPPA*. What it lacks is **prioritization and stagecraft**: 22 is too many to run; some are strong debate-drivers, some are one-voice explainers or overlap; none are tagged with a **lead panelist**, the **cross-seat tension**, a **repo/number anchor**, or a **moderator follow-up**. The task is to **rank these 22 for panel value, route each to the right seat, and enrich them into a moderator-ready guide** — grounded where possible in the repo's reconciled DPPA economics (gates, the empty-window finding, the three canonical cases, the multi-year crossover, the five-line bill).
