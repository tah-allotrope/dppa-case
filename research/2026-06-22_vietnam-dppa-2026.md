# Research Brief: Vietnam DPPA & Power Market 2026 — corrections for the CEBA deck and DPPA web app

**Date:** 2026-06-22
**Modes run:** domain, codebase
**Depth:** deep
**Invocation context:** Correct the numeric assumptions and policy claims in `ceba/CEBA DPPA 2026.pptx` and the `app/` DPPA calculator using up-to-date 2025/2026 sources — Decree 57/2025/ND-CP and amendments, virtual/grid CfD settlement and the five-line bill, FMP averages, EVN retail TOU tariff, DPPA service/balancing fees, loss/price coefficients (k, K_pp), eligibility thresholds, developer financing benchmarks, and real DPPA transaction status.

---

## Synthesis

The **deck is substantially more current than the web app**. The deck's headline reference numbers — retail average **2,204 VND/kWh**, fixed DPPA fees **360 + 163.3 = 523.3 VND/kWh**, loss/price coefficients **k≈1.026 / K_pp≈1.008**, Decree 57/2025 framing — line up with what is publicly verifiable for 2025. The EVN retail average of **2,204.07 VND/kWh** is confirmed exactly (Decision 599/QD-EVN, effective 10 May 2025, +4.8% over the Oct-2024 figure of 2,103.12) [EVN](https://en.evn.com.vn/d/en-US/news/Vietnams-retail-electricity-price-climbs-48-60-163-500704) [DFDL](https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/vietnams-2025-retail-electricity-rates/). So the deck's `2,204` and `523` are good; the app's `retailTariff: 2100`, `assumptions.md` `1,833`, and the single folded `lossFactor: 1.027263` are stale or internally inconsistent and should be brought to the deck's basis.

The **biggest correctness gap in both artifacts is what's NOT verifiable from public sources**: (1) the **average FMP** the deck quotes as ~1,426.6 VND/kWh and the app defaults to 1,700, and (2) the exact 2025 **DPPA service fee (C_DPPA_đv) and difference/balancing fee (P_cl)** values. Vietnamese law now requires EVN to **announce these annually** and publish them on the electricity-market website; C_DPPA_đv = total T&D + system-operation + market + regulatory cost ÷ total commercial output, and P_cl is set per VWEM rules [Reccessary DPPA revisions](https://www.reccessary.com/en/insight/vietnam-dppa-revisions-electricity-market). The 360 / 163.3 / 1,426.6 figures appear to come from EAVCED training material (deck slide 11 cites "EAVCED public training"), not from a published EVN 2025 notice — they should be re-sourced to the official annual EVN/ERAV figures before either artifact claims them as 2026 fact.

Three **2025/2026 developments post-date both artifacts and should be added**, not just corrected: (a) the **competitive wholesale market is now governed by Circular 16/2025/TT-BCT** (1 Feb 2025), amended by **Circular 36/2025/TT-BCT**, with **FMP = SMP + CAN** settled on **30-minute** intervals — both artifacts model hourly, and the app never cites the legal basis [VEPG](https://vepg.vn/legal_doc/circular-36-2025-tt-bct-on-amendments-to-some-articles-of-circular-no-16-2025-tt-bct-dated-february-01-2025-of-the-minister-of-industry-and-trade-on-operation-of-a-competitive-wholesale-electricity/) [EnergyTag](https://energytag.org/vietnams-power-market-shift-what-30-minute-pricing-means-for-clean-energy/); (b) a **two-component retail tariff pilot for large users began Oct 2025** (TC = Cp×Pmax + Ca×Ap), creating a **double-charging risk** where grid cost sits in both the capacity charge and the DPPA service fee — a material new buyer-economics caveat absent from both [Reccessary two-part tariff](https://www.reccessary.com/en/insight/vietnam-dppa-two-part-electricity-tariff-mechanism); (c) **Vietnam's first grid-connected DPPA went live in mid-2026** — Samsung SEVT (Thai Nguyen) ↔ TTC Duc Hue 2 (49 MWp solar + BESS, COD 19 May 2026), ~70 GWh/yr, ~46,000 tCO₂/yr avoided — which upgrades the deck's "first transactions emerging" (slide 52) to a concrete, citable case [Reccessary/Samsung](https://www.reccessary.com/en/news/vietnam-grid-based-dppa-samsung-electronics) [pv-magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/06/03/vietnams-first-direct-power-purchase-agreement-enters-operation/).

[NOTE] Eligibility wording in both artifacts needs softening. Decree 57 **relaxed** Decree 80's rigid "≥200,000 kWh/month AND ≥22 kV" gate: the consumption threshold is **kept for now but made MOIT-adjustable**, the rigid voltage condition is relaxed, and eligibility can be proven via 12-month consumption history (or a first-year commitment for new entrants) [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/b7fae014/decree-57-2025-key-features-and-impact-on-direct-power-purchase-agreements-in-vietnam) [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-renewable-energy-decree-57.html/). The deck's slides 29/38 ("≥22kV only today", "≥200,000 kWh/month") are close but should be reworded to "MOIT-adjustable threshold; voltage condition relaxed under Decree 57."

## Domain: Vietnam DPPA policy & power market 2025/2026

### Discovery
Strongest, most current sources: Norton Rose Fulbright and Baker McKenzie legal analyses of Decree 57/2025 [NRF](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/b7fae014/decree-57-2025-key-features-and-impact-on-direct-power-purchase-agreements-in-vietnam); EVN's own tariff bulletins [EVN](https://en.evn.com.vn/d/en-US/news/Vietnams-retail-electricity-price-climbs-48-60-163-500704); Reccessary's two analyst pieces on DPPA revisions and the two-part-tariff double-charging risk [Reccessary 1](https://www.reccessary.com/en/insight/vietnam-dppa-revisions-electricity-market) [Reccessary 2](https://www.reccessary.com/en/insight/vietnam-dppa-two-part-electricity-tariff-mechanism); EnergyTag on 30-minute pricing [EnergyTag](https://energytag.org/vietnams-power-market-shift-what-30-minute-pricing-means-for-clean-energy/); VEPG legal repository for Circular 16/2025 & 36/2025 [VEPG](https://vepg.vn/legal_doc/circular-36-2025-tt-bct-on-amendments-to-some-articles-of-circular-no-16-2025-tt-bct-dated-february-01-2025-of-the-minister-of-industry-and-trade-on-operation-of-a-competitive-wholesale-electricity/); and reporting on the Samsung first-DPPA [Reccessary/Samsung](https://www.reccessary.com/en/news/vietnam-grid-based-dppa-samsung-electronics).

### Verification
- **Retail 2,204.07 VND/kWh**: confirmed by EVN primary bulletin and corroborated by DFDL — high confidence [EVN](https://en.evn.com.vn/d/en-US/news/Vietnams-retail-electricity-price-climbs-48-60-163-500704) [DFDL](https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/vietnams-2025-retail-electricity-rates/).
- **Decree 57 issued 3 Mar 2025, replacing Decree 80/2024; two models (private wire + grid CfD)**: multiple independent law firms agree — high confidence [NRF](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/b7fae014/decree-57-2025-key-features-and-impact-on-direct-power-purchase-agreements-in-vietnam) [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/energy-mining-infrastructure_1/vietnam-decree-no-572025nd-cp-new-regulations-for-direct-power-sale-and-purchase-dppa-mechanism).
- **Eligibility relaxation**: consistent across NRF and Vietnam Briefing, though they phrase the 200k-kWh threshold slightly differently ("removed" vs. "kept but MOIT-adjustable") — treat as "relaxed/flexible," medium-high confidence [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-renewable-energy-decree-57.html/).
- **FMP = SMP + CAN, 30-min intervals, Circular 16/2025**: confirmed structurally; the **numeric average FMP is not in any accessible public source** — flag as unverified.
- **DPPA fee VND values (360 / 163.3)**: definitions confirmed, exact figures "announced annually by EVN" and not found published — unverified for 2025/2026.

### Comparison
The deck reflects the **Decree-57 grid-CfD virtual DPPA** accurately and is the better-calibrated artifact. The app implements the **same settlement algebra** but with **placeholder calibration** (FMP 1,700 / retail 2,100 / strike 2,100) that predates or ignores the deck's source alignment. Neither artifact yet reflects the **post-Oct-2025 two-component tariff** or the **30-minute market**, which are the most consequential *new* facts for buyer economics. Legal sources diverge mainly on eligibility phrasing, not substance.

### Synthesis
Re-anchor the app to the deck's verified basis (retail 2,204; split k/K_pp; fees 523.3) and re-source the deck's FMP and fee figures to EVN/NSMO primary notices. Add to both: Circular 16/2025 citation, the two-component-tariff double-charging caveat, the Samsung first-transaction case, the relaxed-eligibility wording, and a note that a **Decree 57/58 amendment is pending**.

### Confidence
Medium-High — policy framing and retail tariff are firmly sourced; FMP and exact DPPA fee values remain publicly unverifiable and are the only items that could materially move the calculator.

## Codebase: deck vs. app (mapped in prior turn)

### Discovery
Deck: 57 slides, Modules 1–6 + workshop scenarios 1–5 (`ceba/CEBA DPPA 2026.pptx`). App: `app/src/data/default-scenarios.js` (defaults), `app/src/modules/settlement.js` (five-line math), `app/docs/assumptions.md` + `formulas.md`.

### Verification
Read directly from source files. Confirmed mismatches: (1) `assumptions.md` (retail 1,833 / strike 1,741.35) contradicts `default-scenarios.js` (2,100 / 2,100); (2) `lossFactor 1.027263` is a single folded coefficient and does not equal the deck's k×K_pp = 1.026×1.008 = 1.0342; (3) app `dppaCharge 523.34` correctly equals deck's 360+163.3. Commit `6e0530f "fix: align DPPA defaults with source material"` shows alignment was attempted but the doc was left stale.

### Comparison
The app faithfully implements **Module 2 (five-line bill + cancellation effect)** with live per-hour interactivity the deck lacks; the deck covers the full procurement lifecycle (developer economics, three gates/DSCR, BESS case studies, GHG) the app omits by design. The defect surface is **calibration + stale docs**, not model logic.

### Synthesis
Single source of truth for shared constants (retail, fees, k, K_pp, FMP) should live in one file and feed both `assumptions.md` and `default-scenarios.js`. Fix the doc/code contradiction first (cheap, high-value), then recalibrate FMP/retail/loss to the deck basis, then add the legal-basis and 30-min/two-component caveats as UI footnotes.

### Confidence
High — claims are read straight from the repository.
