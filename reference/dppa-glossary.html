DPPA Glossary

The nomenclature of Vietnam's virtual DPPA settlement. Symbols match the CEBA deck and the live engine in app/src/modules/settlement.js.

Symbols

SymbolReads asMeaning
FMPfull market priceWholesale spot price the generator is paid per kWh (VWEM). FMP = SMP + CAN. 2025 ref ~1,427 VND/kWh — illustrative (NSMO/ERAV not public).
Strike / P_cstrike priceThe fixed CfD reference price the factory and developer agree. The one big negotiable number.
kprice conversionConverts FMP to the customer delivery point. ~1.026. A price adjustment, not a volume loss.
K_pploss factorVoltage-level loss factor (~1.008 at 110 kV). Converts generator-meter output to buyer-credited volume.
k × K_pploss factor (combined)= 1.026 × 1.008 = 1.0342. Multiplies market energy in line 1. (App: lossFactor.)
Q_metermetered generationkWh measured at the generator's meter.
Q_adjadjusted volume= Q_meter / K_pp — delivered volume credited at your meter.
Q_Khcsettled volume= min(Load, Q_adj) — the renewable volume actually received & consumed. Lines 1–3 bill on this.
Q_ccontracted volumeCfD contracted quantity. Above real consumption it buys nothing.
Q_CfDCfD volume= min(Q_c, Q_Khc) — CfD settles only on received/consumed volume.
C_dppa_dvDPPA service feeFixed 360 VND/kWh on matched volume (regulated).
P_clbalancing feeFixed 163.3 VND/kWh on matched volume (regulated).
523.3fixed fees360 + 163.3 = 523.3 VND/kWh — fixed every matched kWh, not negotiable. (App: dppaCharge.)
P1retail tariffEVN average retail price for residual/shortfall purchase. 2025: 2,204 VND/kWh.
BAUbusiness as usualYour current EVN bill, escalated ~4%/yr — the benchmark every DPPA is judged against.
DSCRdebt service coverageCash available for debt service ÷ debt service. Lender gate: ≥ ~1.20x every year.
TOUtime of useOff-peak / normal / peak retail pricing. Peak ≈ 3.4× off-peak.

The five-line bill (the spine of everything)

1. Market energy = Q_Khc × FMP × k × K_pp
2. DPPA service fee = Q_Khc × 360
3. Balancing fee = Q_Khc × 163.3
4. Residual = (Load − Q_Khc) × P1 (retail TOU)
   C_EVN = lines 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 (billed by EVN)
5. CfD settlement = (Strike − FMP) × Q_CfD (bilateral; + or −)
   C_KH = C_EVN + C_CfD (your total cost)
Sign convention CfD positive (FMP < Strike) → factory pays developer. CfD negative (FMP > Strike) → developer pays factory.

From app/src/data/default-scenarios.js & deck-qa/consolidation-map.md. Retail 2,204 · fees 523.3 · loss 1.0342 are verified; FMP ~1,427 & strike 2,000 are illustrative teaching values.
