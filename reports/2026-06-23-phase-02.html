Math / Algorithm Used

Representative-day × 365 annualisation

The settlement engine operates on a single 24-hour profile. To project annual economics we treat that profile as a representative day and multiply by 365:

annualBau_n  = calculateSettlement(yearInputs).totals.baselineCost × 365
annualDppa_n = calculateSettlement(yearInputs).totals.totalCost    × 365

Teaching simplification: real DPPA projects would use a full 8,760-hour profile. The 365× scaling preserves the shape relationships while keeping the workshop app fast and interactive.

Escalation compounding (geometric, year-start-anchored)

For year n ∈ [1 .. years]:
  evnFactor    = (1 + evnEscalation)^(n − 1)   // Year 1 → factor = 1
  strikeFactor = (1 + strikeEscalation)^(n − 1)
  retailTariff_n = retailTariff_base × evnFactor
  strikePrice_n  = strikePrice_base  × strikeFactor
  // FMP held flat (market price stays constant — teaching simplification)
  yearInputs = { ...baseInputs, retailTariff: retailTariff_n, strikePrice: strikePrice_n }

Year 1 is the base year (factor = 1.0), matching the teaching convention "Year 1 numbers match the slider values on screen." Year 2 is the first compounded year.

Crossover detection

crossoverIdx = yearlyData.findIndex(y => y.cumSavings > 0)
crossoverYear = crossoverIdx === -1 ? null : crossoverIdx + 1

Cumulative savings = cumulative BAU − cumulative DPPA. The crossover year is when DPPA has paid back its Year-1 premium in total. Null means the horizon is too short for payback at the given escalation rates.

Limitations / Second-Best Alternative

Representative-day simplification. Real projects use full hourly (or 30-minute) annual profiles to account for seasonality, weekday/weekend patterns, and plant availability. The 365× multiplier is accurate for teaching but will overstate or understate actual economics for plants with strong seasonal output profiles (e.g., a rooftop plant with summer-heavy yield).
FMP held flat across years. The model does not escalate FMP. In reality, the spot price will move with fuel costs, hydro availability, and Vietnam's increasing renewable penetration. Holding FMP flat makes the CfD developer payment mechanical but understates market-price risk in out-years.
Alternative considered: A separate projectMultiYear that ran the settlement engine once and then scaled outputs by year rather than re-running per year. Rejected — re-running per year is marginally slower but avoids subtle bugs from incorrect linear extrapolation of non-linear settlement outcomes (e.g., when shortfall/excess shifts as profiles overlap differently).
